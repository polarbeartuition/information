The name

Why Polar Bear?

Polar bears are some of the most remarkable animals on the planet. Not because things come easy to them, but because of how they face difficulty. These are the qualities we aim to reflect in every session.

Perseverance
Polar bears cover hundreds of kilometres through brutal conditions without stopping. We teach students to keep working through the hard problems, not around them.
Focus
A polar bear hunting is one of nature's most patient creatures. It waits, watches, and acts at exactly the right moment. We build that same focus in how students approach exam questions.
Adaptability
Polar bears thrive in one of the most demanding environments on earth by adapting to what is in front of them. We teach students to read new problem types and think on their feet, not just recall rehearsed answers.
Resilience
Getting a question wrong is not a setback. It is information. Polar bears do not abandon the hunt after one miss. We help students treat mistakes the same way: calmly, and as part of the process.

PB stands for Personal Best. That is always the goal.

Approach

How I teach
differently

Competitive exams don't reward memorisation. They reward the student who has genuinely understood how to think. My whole approach is built around that principle.

I sat the same exams my students face. I know what the questions are really testing, and I know how to build those skills from the ground up rather than just drilling past papers.

01
Understanding before speed
Concepts are consolidated before we practise under time pressure. Speed without understanding creates fragile performance on the day.
02
Targeted gap-filling
Every student starts with a diagnostic. We work through gaps systematically with no time wasted covering what they already know.
03
Exam instinct, not just knowledge
Students learn to read questions, manage their time, eliminate wrong answers, and check their work. That's how knowledge becomes marks.
04
Confidence as an outcome
The best results come from students who walk in feeling genuinely ready. Building that confidence is part of the preparation, not an afterthought.
